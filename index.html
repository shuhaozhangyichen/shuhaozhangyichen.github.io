Research interests

DYNAMICS

Independence & entropy

Independence, sequence entropy, and mean sensitivity in topological and measure-theoretic dynamics; ergodicity of frame flows.

ERGODIC THEORY

Recurrence & averages

Recurrence along Hardy-field sequences, Bohr obstructions, Khintchin averages, and orbit multiplicity.

CONNECTIONS

Combinatorics & fractals

Sumset configurations, combinatorial number theory, and fractal transference through continued fractions.
